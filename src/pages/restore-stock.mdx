What this can do:
- Restore stock LightOS from this guide's LightOS with root or LineageOS.
- Restore the active slot, including the original system image when returning from LineageOS.
- Optionally relock the bootloader after verifying the restoration.

Requirements:
- Light Phone III (TLP301, firmware 00WW_1_440000).
- Unlocked bootloader.
- Root and Shell access granted in ReSukiSU.
- Desktop Chrome or Edge.

<Warning>
Returning from LineageOS and optional relocking erase your data. Back up first. Complete the recovery factory reset when prompted.
</Warning>
